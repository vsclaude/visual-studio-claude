# Vey Pro

Every Claude account is **free** — claude.ai plans, Console, SSO, as many as
you have, **Continue with …** between them, and
[custom API endpoints](/docs/accounts#adding-a-custom-api). Free works in **one
project**; **Pro** adds as many as you have. Pro is bought **once**, by bank
transfer, for one, three or five devices. There is no subscription and nothing
to renew, and no account to sign in to.

## Free or Pro

The pill at the right of the title bar says which one you are on — **Free** or
**Pro** — and opens the Pro dialog. On Free, **Add project** a second time
opens the Pro dialog instead of the folder picker; projects you already had
keep working.

## What Pro adds

- **Every project.** Free works in one; Pro adds as many as you have, each
  with its own chats, files and terminals.

Everything else in the app is free: every Claude account, custom API
endpoints, many chats at once, git, the editor, the terminal, search, and
every update.

## Plans

Pro is bought once, for the number of devices you work on:

| Plan      | Price     | Devices at once |
| --------- | --------- | --------------- |
| 1 Device  | 99.000 ₫  | 1               |
| 3 Devices | 199.000 ₫ | 3               |
| 5 Devices | 299.000 ₫ | 5               |

Every plan includes every update, forever, and a refund within **7 days** —
reply to your licence email with its order code.

## Getting Pro

There is no account to create and nothing to sign in to.

1. Click **Upgrade** at the right of the title bar.
2. Enter the email your licence key should be sent to.
3. Click the plan you want. A page with a VietQR opens in your browser — scan
   it in your banking app, keeping the transfer's content exactly as shown.

Pro turns on for this computer the moment the transfer arrives, and the
**licence key** — `VEY-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX` — is mailed to you.

## Pro on another computer

Open Vey Studio there, click **Upgrade**, paste the key from your email under
**Have a key?** and click **Activate**. A key works on as many computers at
once as its plan's devices.

Moving to a new computer? Open the Vey Pro dialog on the old one and click
**Deactivate this device** to free its seat, then activate the new one. The
dialog on any activated computer shows the key, to copy.
