# Claude accounts

Plain Claude Code ties a conversation to one login and stops it at that
login's limit. Vey Studio lets you sign in to as many Claude accounts as you
have, and move a chat between them without losing it.

## Adding an account

1. At the foot of the chats column, under the project's chats, find
   **Accounts** and click **+** beside it.
2. Give the account a name — _Work_, _Personal_, _Team_.
3. Sign in. It is Claude Code's own login, so your password never passes
   through Vey Studio.

The `claude` you already use is listed first, as **This account**. Each account
you add is kept in a directory of its own and stays signed in. Every row says
whether it is signed in, and as whom. Open a row's menu to **Check again**, or
to **Sign in…** again (or **Sign in as another account…** on one that is
already signed in).

Each account row also shows how much of its five-hour and weekly limit is
used. The **Credits** row at the bottom is for chats on credits; **Sign in**
there signs in to Vey Studio.

![The Accounts section at the foot of the chats column: This account with its five-hour and weekly meters, and Credits](/screenshots/accounts.png)

## Choosing the account a chat runs on

Every chat has an **Account** menu in its composer. Pick an account there
before you send, and the chat runs on it.

## Switching a chat to another account

When an account reaches its limit, open the chat's **Account** menu and pick
another one. The conversation moves with it: your next message carries on with
everything said so far — no pasting, no starting over.

If every account is out of its window, pick **Pay with credits** in the same
menu. See [Credits](/docs/credits).

::: info
Switching is always your choice. Vey Studio never moves a chat to another
account, or onto credits, by itself.
:::

## How much of a plan is left

Beside the composer, a meter shows how much of the account's five-hour window
has been used. Click it for every window, including the weekly one.
